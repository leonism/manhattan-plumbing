What to do in a Plumbing Emergency

01

Stay Calm

Assess the situation carefully and keep everyone away from the area.

02

Shut Off Water

Turn off the main water valve if possible to prevent further damage.

03

Open Taps

Open outside taps to drain remaining water from the pipes.

04

Call Us

Contact our emergency line immediately for professional help.
