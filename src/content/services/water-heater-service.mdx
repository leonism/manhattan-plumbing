# Professional Water Heater Services in Manhattan

Hot water is a necessity of modern life, and when your water heater fails, it can disrupt your entire household or business. Manhattan Plumbing provides expert repair, installation, and maintenance services for all types of water heating systems.

Whether you have a traditional gas tank, an electric unit, or a high-efficiency tankless system, our licensed technicians have the specialized training needed to diagnose problems and recommend the most cost-effective solutions.

### Should You Repair or Replace?

Most water heaters have a lifespan of 10-15 years. If your unit is approaching this age or experiencing frequent breakdowns, a replacement might save you money in the long run through improved energy efficiency and reliability. We can help you compare models and choose the perfect system for your needs.

**Signs your water heater needs attention:**
- Discolored or rusty water
- Unusual noises coming from the tank
- Inconsistent water temperature
- Leaks around the base of the unit
- Age over 10 years
