# Modern Materials for a Better Home

Plumbing technology has evolved. We help you choose the right materials for your specific needs, balancing performance, cost, and longevity.

- **Copper**: Durable, naturally antimicrobial, and recyclable.
- **PEX**: Flexible, freeze-resistant, and cost-effective.
- **PVC/CPVC**: Ideal for drains and chemical resistance.

## Professional Pipe Repair & Replacement

When your home's piping system fails, it can lead to catastrophic water damage and costly repairs. At Manhattan Plumbing, we specialize in both emergency repairs and planned infrastructure upgrades. Whether you're dealing with a single leak or need a complete building repiping, our master plumbers bring the expertise and precision required for high-performance Manhattan properties.

Our approach combines traditional craftsmanship with modern leak detection and piping materials, ensuring that once we fix a problem, it stays fixed for decades.
