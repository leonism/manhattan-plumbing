# Luxury Kitchen & Bath Remodeling in Manhattan

A major renovation is the perfect time to ensure your plumbing infrastructure is modern, efficient, and capable of supporting your new high-end fixtures. At Manhattan Plumbing, we specialize in the complex plumbing requirements of New York City residential and commercial remodels.

From luxury brownstone renovations to modern high-rise kitchen updates, our team brings a high level of craftsmanship to every project. We understand that in remodeling, what's behind the walls is just as important as the beautiful finishes on the outside.

### Our Remodeling Process

We work closely with your architects, interior designers, and general contractors to ensure the plumbing phase of your project is executed flawlessly and on schedule.

1.  **Consultation & Design:** We review your plans and suggest optimizations for drainage and water supply.
2.  **Permitting:** We navigate the complex NYC DOB permitting process for you.
3.  **Rough-in Plumbing:** Expert installation of all internal piping, venting, and drainage.
4.  **Fixture Installation:** Careful installation of your high-end faucets, showers, tubs, and appliances.
5.  **Final Inspection:** We ensure everything meets or exceeds building codes and your expectations.
